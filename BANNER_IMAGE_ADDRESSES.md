## How to use this

Every school already has a banner photo. To swap one, paste an image address into its **New image address** cell: right-click the photo and choose "Copy image address" (it usually ends in .jpg or .png). Leave a row blank to keep the current photo.

- **Notes**: use this column to reposition a photo instead of replacing it, e.g. "move up, too much sky".
- **Credit**: if the image comes from Wikimedia Commons, Flickr or a university site, add the page link too so the author credit and license can be checked.
